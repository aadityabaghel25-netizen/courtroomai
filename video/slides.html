The problem · Supreme Court of India, 2 July 2026

Citing AI judgments without verification is now misconduct.

“It is a misconduct on the part of an advocate to cite such judgments without verification.”

AI research tools can invent case law: a case name that sounds real, a citation that looks real, a quote no judge ever wrote. Stanford researchers found legal AI tools hallucinate in 1 out of 6 or more queries.

Pooja Ramesh Singh v. Jammu and Kashmir Bank Ltd., Civil Appeal No. 11950 of 2025, para 7 · Stanford RegLab / HAI, 2024
And since 1 July 2024

India's criminal codes have new section numbers.

IPC→Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)
CrPC→Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)
Evidence Act→Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA)

Which code applies depends on the date of the offence. One wrong number is an easy, costly mistake.
